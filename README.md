# Mumbai Retail Shop Closure Analysis

## Problem Statement
Why are retail shops in Mumbai shutting down?
This project analyses 50,000 shops across 30 areas of Mumbai
to find patterns behind shop closures.

## Dataset
- 50,000 shops across 30 Mumbai areas
- 12 variables: Area, Shop Type, Status, Rent, Google Rating,
  Metro Proximity, Competitors, Years in Business

## Key Findings
1. 1 in 3 Mumbai shops has closed (33.6% closure rate)
2. Clothing shops close at 62% — highest of all categories
3. Shops paying above ₹35,000/month rent close at double the rate
4. Poor Google ratings (below 3.0) predict closure 3x more
5. Metro proximity doesn't help — higher rent cancels footfall benefit
6. Pharmacy, grocery, tailoring are the most resilient shop types
